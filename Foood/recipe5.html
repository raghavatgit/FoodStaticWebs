Chocolate Cake

Soft, moist and rich chocolate cake that melts in your mouth.

⏱ Time: 50 mins
🔥 Difficulty: Medium
🍽 Servings: 4–6

Chocolate cake is a timeless dessert loved by everyone. This recipe produces a soft, moist, and rich cake with deep chocolate flavor, perfect for celebrations or simply satisfying your sweet cravings at home.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup all-purpose flour
  • 1/2 cup cocoa powder
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 1/2 cup butter or oil
  • 2 eggs
  • 1/2 cup milk
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 1 tsp vanilla essence

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 180°C for 10 minutes.
  2. Mix flour, cocoa powder, and baking powder.
  3. In another bowl, whisk eggs, sugar, and butter.
  4. Add dry ingredients gradually and mix well.
  5. Pour in milk and vanilla essence.
  6. Transfer batter to a greased baking pan.
  7. Bake for 30–35 minutes until fully cooked.
  8. Let it cool, slice, and serve.
