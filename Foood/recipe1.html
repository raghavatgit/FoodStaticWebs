White Sauce Pasta

Creamy, rich and comforting Italian-style pasta perfect for any occasion.

⏱ Time: 25 mins
🔥 Difficulty: Easy
🍽 Servings: 2

White Sauce Pasta is a classic creamy dish made with a smooth béchamel sauce, combined with perfectly cooked pasta and seasoned vegetables. This recipe is beginner-friendly and delivers restaurant-quality taste at home.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup pasta (penne or fusilli)
  • 2 cups milk
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 1 tbsp all-purpose flour
  • 1/2 cup grated cheese
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • Mixed vegetables (optional)

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Boil pasta in salted water until al dente. Drain and set aside.
  2. In a pan, melt butter and add flour. Cook for 1–2 minutes.
  3. Slowly add milk while stirring to avoid lumps.
  4. Cook until the sauce thickens.
  5. Add cheese, salt, and pepper. Mix well.
  6. Add boiled pasta and vegetables. Toss evenly.
  7. Serve hot with extra cheese on top.
