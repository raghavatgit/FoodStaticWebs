Healthy Salad

A refreshing mix of fresh vegetables, packed with nutrients and natural flavors.

⏱ Time: 15 mins
🔥 Difficulty: Easy
🍽 Servings: 2

This healthy salad is a perfect combination of fresh vegetables, offering a light, nutritious, and refreshing meal option. It’s quick to prepare and ideal for those looking for a wholesome and balanced diet.

Ingredients

  • Cucumber (sliced)
  • Tomato (chopped)
  • Onion (sliced)
  • Carrot (grated)
  • Lettuce leaves
  • Olive oil
  • Lemon juice
  • Salt & black pepper

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Wash and chop all vegetables properly.
  2. Add them to a large mixing bowl.
  3. Drizzle olive oil and lemon juice.
  4. Add salt and black pepper to taste.
  5. Toss everything well to combine.
  6. Serve fresh for best taste.
