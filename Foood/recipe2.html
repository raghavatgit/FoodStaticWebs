Cheese Burger

Juicy grilled patty layered with melted cheese, fresh veggies, and soft buns.

⏱ Time: 30 mins
🔥 Difficulty: Medium
🍽 Servings: 2

The classic cheese burger is a perfect combination of a juicy grilled patty, melted cheese, and crisp vegetables. This recipe delivers a restaurant-style burger at home with simple ingredients and easy steps.

Ingredients

  • 2 burger buns
  • 2 patties (veg or chicken)
  • 2 cheese slices
  • Lettuce leaves
  • Tomato slices
  • Onion rings
  • Mayonnaise / ketchup
  • Butter for toasting

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Toast burger buns with butter until golden brown.
  2. Grill or pan-fry the patties until fully cooked.
  3. Place cheese slice on the patty and let it melt.
  4. Spread mayonnaise or ketchup on buns.
  5. Layer lettuce, patty, tomato, and onion.
  6. Cover with the top bun and press lightly.
  7. Serve hot with fries or dip.
