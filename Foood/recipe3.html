Veg Pizza

Crispy crust topped with fresh vegetables, rich tomato sauce, and melted cheese.

⏱ Time: 40 mins
🔥 Difficulty: Medium
🍽 Servings: 2–3

Veg Pizza is a crowd-favorite dish featuring a crispy base layered with tangy tomato sauce, colorful vegetables, and gooey melted cheese. This homemade version delivers authentic flavor with simple ingredients and is perfect for family meals or gatherings.

Ingredients

  • 1 pizza base
  • 1/2 cup pizza sauce
  • 1 cup mozzarella cheese
  • Capsicum (sliced)
  • Onion (sliced)
  • Tomato (sliced)
  • Olives (optional)
  • Oregano & chili flakes

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 200°C for 10 minutes.
  2. Spread pizza sauce evenly on the base.
  3. Add a layer of cheese.
  4. Place all vegetables evenly on top.
  5. Sprinkle more cheese and seasoning.
  6. Bake for 12–15 minutes until crust is crispy.
  7. Slice and serve hot.
